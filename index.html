Which test will you take?

It depends on the date you filed Form N-400. The app covers both versions, and you pick yours when you start.

Filed before October 20, 2025

2008 civics test

100 questions

Filed on or after October 20, 2025

2025 civics test

128 questions

Not sure? Check the USCIS 2025 civics test page.
